## What needs a backend

This page is the public, no-account front end. Some features in the full NEXORA plan cannot be done honestly in a static page.

Accounts, profiles and synced progress

Registration, login and cross-device sync need a server and database with secure authentication. Today, favorites, saved prompts and lesson progress are stored in your browser only.

Admin dashboard and moderation

Publishing, user-submission review and analytics must be protected by server-side roles, not hidden buttons. They are not included here.

Community and live AI responses

A community needs moderation, guidelines and privacy controls. Live AI features need an API key held on a server. Until those exist, the Lab is a template builder, not an AI.

News and fast-changing information

This page shows no news feed on purpose. A curated editorial workflow with real dates and authors is required first.
